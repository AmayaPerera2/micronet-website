Our story

From one company to a connected group.

A practical journey built around client operations, capable people, and technology services.

1992

Founded in Colombo

Micronet Group began under Micronet Information System (Pvt) Ltd and established its place in Sri Lanka’s information technology services sector.

Scope

Operations, not only technology

The organisation expanded across BPO/KPO, contact centres, customer care, marketing services, and other ancillary services for local and global companies.

People

Teams placed where the work is

Dedicated teams are deployed with client organisations and work to the agreed scope, hours, payment terms, and deployment arrangements.

Today

Scale with the same standard

More than 2,000 employees have been deployed across 30 companies in banking, FMCG, travel, insurance, manufacturing, hotels, and technology.
