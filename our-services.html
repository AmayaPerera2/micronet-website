Staff Outsourcing

Micronet transfers people with the required profile, competencies, and skills for a particular job or activity, and deploys them at the client’s site.

Required profiles and skillsClient-site deploymentAgreed working hoursReplacement support
How deployment works

The client and Micronet agree the services, scope, payment terms, and deployment. Deployed people work to the client’s hours and the rendered agreement. If a client is dissatisfied, or in the case of absenteeism, Micronet replaces the employee.
